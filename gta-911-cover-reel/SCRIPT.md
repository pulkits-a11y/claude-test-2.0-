# GTA 9/11 Cover Reel: voiceover script

Runtime: 35 s of voiceover, 37.7 s with the final hold.

## Full script

What if I told you 9/11 is the reason GTA covers look like this?

This was meant to be the GTA III cover. Six months of painting. It had guns, fire, and burning buildings all over it.

Then 9/11 hit, six weeks before launch. Rockstar pulled it in the US.

The new cover couldn't show guns, buildings, or fire. Artist Stephen Bliss had about a week, so he chopped up his own painting. He only kept the faces, the cars, and the helicopter, and boxed them into a grid.

That rush job? It's been the template for every GTA cover since. Vice City, San Andreas, IV, V, even GTA VI.

Same boxes. Same grid. 25 years later.
