# Typography plan (whole-video look: Stepped)

Stepped: every text animation updates on 3-frame steps (10 fps feel), light film grain over the frame (seed stepped at 10 fps), slow stepped drift (100->103%) on held Text Centerpieces. Fonts: Archivo variable with explicit axes (captions wght 640/wdth 100; small TC words wght 520; WHAT HAPPENED? wght 880/wdth 80) and Instrument Serif Regular/Italic for keywords. The typography guide's three BTW effects (turbulent displace, displacement map, noise inside the text) run as one custom shader on all display type, never on captions.
