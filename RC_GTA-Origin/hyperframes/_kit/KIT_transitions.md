The RC_GTA-Origin kit's transitions area: `transitions.css`, `transitions.js` (exposes `RCK.transitions`) and this file.
Source of truth: Master Edit Plan p.15-16 "Transitions (X1-X9)" and "Into each beat", the beat builds p.19-28, the

## Rules every X item follows

* `RCK.transitions.<helper>(tl, target, atSeconds, opts) -> endSeconds`. `target` = selector or Element. Times snap to
  the 30 fps grid; pass `frame / 30` (composition-local frame = beat frame + 6, the head handle).
* **Elements are written in your HTML.** Helpers only add untimed children (slats, leak layers, the X4 canvas,
  the X9 fill) and animate: the `opacity` of the element you pass, and transforms/colours of its children or of a
  plain untimed wrapper. Never put `class="clip"` on a wrapper that holds a timed `<video>` (X2, X4 views, X6 world):
  time the `<video>`, keep the wrapper untimed. X3/X7/X9 elements may be untimed (invisible at opacity 0 outside
  their frames) or a `.clip` that spans at least the transition frames.
* **Hard changes** land ON frame n: a 0.5-frame tween between frames n-1 and n, so the render shows the new state on
  frame n whatever order frames are seeked in. Start states are written with `gsap.set` when the helper is called;
  call each helper once per element (exception: `sirenWash` may be called several times on one element).
* **Write `data-start`/`data-duration` with `RCK.type.attr()/dur()`** (floored to 1e-4 s; frame n is drawn at exactly
  n/30 s, a value rounded up starts a frame late).
* **No CSS filter on footage.** X6's motion blur on `<video>/<img>` uses the media-treatment blur control
  (`--hf-color-grading-blur`, the documented seek-safe animation contract); CSS blur only on non-media DOM. X4's RGB
  split is drawn in its own canvas (still mode) or comes from media-treatment `chromaticAberration` (live views).
* **Blend modes need the picture in the same composition.** X7 (Screen) and X9 (Overlay) blend with what is under
  them *inside this composition*. Over a transparent area that Tesseract fills later they have nothing to blend
  with and render as a plain tint in the alpha MOV (wrong look). See the B14 note under X9.
* Colours: only base.css variables (`--white`, `--ink-black`, `--warm-glow`, `--siren-red`, `--siren-blue`).

## X2 Cut, then drift

**Plan:** "After the cut, the incoming clip slides 40-80 px sideways over 20f, ease out." Calmer history moments; B3, B6.

**Values:** 60 px (**kit choice**, middle of 40-80; any value 40-80 via `px`, a warning outside), 20f, `power3.out`
(the plan's "ease out" translation; GSAP power3 is quartic). The clip lands on its designed position: it starts `px`
off and slides to 0. `dir:'left'` = moves right-to-left (starts at +px), `'right'` = starts at -px.

**Markup:** wrap the incoming content in an untimed wrapper (it may hold timed videos/text):
```html
<div id="b06-card" class="clip rc-card rc-full" data-start="2.2333" data-duration="3.5333" style="z-index:1"></div>
<div id="b06-x2" class="rc-x2" style="z-index:3">                      <!-- untimed, GSAP x -->

  <div id="b06-t8" class="clip rc-t8 rc-t8--right rc-on-card" data-start="2.2333" ...>...</div>
</div>
```
**Call:** `X.drift(tl, '#b06-x2', 67/30, { px: 60, dir: 'left' })` -> `87/30`.

**Frames:** cut frame C shows the clip at +60 px; C+k shows `60*(1-k/20)^4`: C+1 48.9, C+5 19.0, C+10 3.8, C+20 0.
Measured in the demo to 0.1 px (`review/X2_drift_*`).

**Bleed:** the clip's trailing edge moves 60 px, so whatever is under the wrapper shows in that strip during the drift.
Keep a uniform background static under it (B6: the #E9E9E9 card is its own static layer, only the clip + quote
drift), or give footage >= 60 px of overscan on that side.

**Beats:** B6 (b06-dull) "X2 into the L5 gray card" on "One", beat f61 = local f67, lands f81 (beat).
B3 (b03-dundee) "X2 into the band" on "Dundee," beat f58: the L2 Dundee shot is Tesseract footage

## X4 Pixel-block tear

**Plan:** "6-10f: the frame splits into 16 x 9 blocks that jump +/-30 px, some freezing on the old frame, with a 6 px
RGB split, then cut." **Bug beats only: B2, B8, B17** (plus B16 is a bug beat but has no X4). Never elsewhere.

**Values:** 16 x 9 blocks over the tear region (120 x 120 px on a full frame); every frame each block gets a new
role from one `RCK.rng(seed)` stream (same seed = same tear, frame for frame): **jump** (p = 0.7 of the non-frozen blocks, **kit choice**) = displaced by integer
dx, dy uniform in [-30, +30] px; **freeze** = in place on the old frame, a block that freezes stays frozen (25% of the
blocks by the last frame, **kit choice**); the rest stay in place. RGB split 6 px = red +6 px, blue -6 px (the T10
convention, **kit choice** for the direction), applied to the whole torn picture AFTER the tear (displaced block
edges fringe; flat areas do not), clamped and clipped to the region. `frames` 6-10 (warning outside). Then cut:
from frame at+frames nothing of the tear is drawn (`end:'cut'`), or it holds the last torn frame (`end:'hold'`).

Two sources:
* **(a) still mode** (recommended for B8, B17, and B2 with a hold): a still of the old frame (the composition's own
  frame at the tear start, captions hidden) is decoded once, split into R/G/B planes and torn in a canvas. The torn
  picture covers the region: jumping blocks over the frozen old frame (default), or with `gap:'var(--ink-black)'`
  only frozen + jumping blocks over #0A0A0A (non-jumping, non-frozen blocks drop out: a shatter).
* **(b) live mode:** add `.rc-x4__view` copies of the live picture (each with a timed `<video>` = same `src`,
  `data-start`, `data-duration`, `data-media-start` as the original). Per frame the jumping blocks are dealt to the
  K views (K = number of views; one shared dx/dy per view per frame, clip-path windows); frozen blocks come from the
  still (if any, already split); the rest shows your original picture under the container. Split on the views:
  media-treatment `chromaticAberration` = `6 * 50 / W`, `chromaticAngle` 0.5 (red right), W = the view's DISPLAYED
  media width (**calibrated**: px = value x W / 50; 16:9 footage `object-fit: cover` in a 600 x 450 box is 800 wide
  -> 0.375). In hold mode the live views keep playing inside their frozen blocks; use still mode for a true freeze.

**Markup** (the container is untimed, positioned over the region; it clips to its own box):
```html
<!-- (a) still mode, full frame -->
<div id="b08-x4" class="rc-x4" style="z-index:30">
  <img class="rc-x4__still" src="assets/x4/b08-old.png" alt="">     <!-- old frame, container size -->
</div>
<!-- (b) live mode on a region (e.g. a screen rect 600 x 450 at 660,300) -->
<div id="b02-x4" class="rc-x4" style="left:660px; top:300px; width:600px; height:450px; z-index:30">
  <img class="rc-x4__still" src="assets/x4/b02-old.png" alt="">     <!-- crop of the region, for frozen blocks -->
  <div class="rc-x4__view"><video id="b02-x4v1" class="clip" src="assets/footage/R9.1.mp4" muted playsinline
       data-start="..." data-duration="..." data-media-start="..."></video></div>   <!-- x2 or x3 -->
</div>
```
**Call:** `X.tear(tl, '#b08-x4', 41/30, { frames: 10, seed: 8 })` -> `51/30` (cut to black there).
`X.tear(tl, '#b02-x4', 107/30, { frames: 8, end: 'hold', until: 133/30 })`.
Options: `grid:[x,y,w,h]` (block grid inside the container, default all of it), `cols`, `rows`, `jump`, `rgb`,
`freeze`, `pJump`, `seed` (default from the id), `gap`, `end`, `until` (hold end; default the nearest timed
ancestor's end, else for good).

**Making the still** (as the demo's `tools/make_stills.sh` does): build the beat without the tear, then
`npx --yes hyperframes@0.9.52 snapshot . --at <(tearFrame/30)+0.00005> --no-end -o .stills` with captions hidden
(`data-hidden` on them for that run), copy the PNG to `assets/x4/` (crop it to the container with
`ffmpeg -i in.png -vf crop=W:H:X:Y out.png` for a region). The still must be the container's size.

**Timing:** frame `at` is the first torn frame (the old frame torn); frames at .. at+frames-1 are torn; at+frames is
the cut (your next clip's `data-start`). The canvas is redrawn from the timeline (GSAP `onUpdate` of a tween spanning
the tear, a pure function of the frame), and a `window.__hf.buildReady` promise makes the first seek wait for the
still. Seek-safe (verified in snapshots in any order).

**Beats:**
* B2 (b02-bug) on "broke" beat f101 = local f107, 8f, "on the CRT picture only", then (STORYBOARD interp) the picture
  holds torn while the error box pops at beat f109. Recipe: still mode, container full frame, `grid` = the screen's
  bounding box, container `style="clip-path: polygon(x1px y1px, x2px y2px, x3px y3px, x4px y4px)"` = the screen quad
  so blocks never cover the bezel, still = the composition's own frame at f107 (CRT look included), `end:'hold'`.
* B8 (b08-break) on "breaks" beat f35 = local f41, 10f (beat 35-45), then the hard cut to black #0A0A0A at beat f45
  (= S07's end / MU4 mute start). The stamped L5 card is the old frame. `gap:'var(--ink-black)'` gives the
  "shatters" read (optional).
* B17 (b17-loop) final tear on the GTA 6 logo beat f170 = local f176, 6f (beat 170-176), then black. The T10 "now?"
  text is separate (type kit); keep it above the tear container.

**Limitations:** still mode freezes the picture under the tear for its 6-10 frames (by design). In still mode with
`gap`, and in live mode, the uncovered parts are not RGB split. A corner-pinned region needs the clip-path
polygon (the grid itself is axis-aligned).

## X5 Blinds wipe

**Plan:** "12 vertical slats rotate shut on the old shot and open on the new one, 8f each way." Chapter turns; B3, B15.

**Values:** 12 slats of 160 px (1920/12) in ink black `#0A0A0A` (**kit choice**: the plan names no slat colour; ink
black is the palette's colour for black screens and turning points). Each slat rotates about its own vertical centre
line (`rotationY`, perspective 1000 px): +90 (edge-on, invisible) -> 0 (shut, frame covered) -> -90 (open again).
Constant angular speed, 11.25 deg a frame (**kit choice**: one continuous rotation through the shut point, so the two
halves rendered by two compositions join without a speed jump, and all 8 frames of each half visibly move).
Coverage = cos(angle), measured to 0.5% (`review/X5_blinds_*`): B-8 0 | B-7 19.5% | B-6 38% | B-5 56% | B-4 71% |
B-3 83% | B-2 92% | B-1 98% | **B 100% (shut)** | B+1 98% | ... | B+7 19.5% | B+8 0 (slats removed).

**Split across the boundary** (PRODUCTION_SPEC: the outgoing composition plays the out half up to the boundary frame,
the incoming plays the in half from it; handles hold the closing / opening state):

| composition | element | frames it owns |
|---|---|---|
| outgoing (B2, B14) | `.rc-x5` clip from B-8 to its clip end (tail handle) | B-8 .. B-1 closing, B .. B+5 (handle) shut |
| incoming (B3, B15) | `.rc-x5` clip from 0 (head handle) to >= B+8 | B-6 .. B (handle + boundary) shut, B+1 .. B+7 opening, gone at B+8 |

The boundary frame B itself (the incoming beat's f0) is shut in both compositions, so the trim point can move by the
handle without a gap.

**Markup:** `<div id="b02-x5" class="clip rc-x5" data-start="..." data-duration="..." style="z-index:50"></div>`
(slats are added by the helper; z above everything the slats must cover, captions included).
**Call:** outgoing `X.blindsClose(tl, '#b02-x5', 186/30)` (b02 local boundary = 6 + 180) -> 186/30.
incoming `X.blindsOpen(tl, '#b03-x5', 6/30)` -> 14/30. Use one `.rc-x5` element per half.

**Beats:** B2 -> B3 (b02 local 178-186 closing; b03 local 6-14 opening onto the T3 year card on "Here's how").
B14 -> B15 (b14-since local 186-194 closing over the escape shot; b15-proof local 6-14 opening onto the L7 arcade).
B14's picture is Tesseract footage: the slats are opaque, so they work in the alpha MOV.

## X6 Zoom through a screen

**Plan:** "Scale into (or back out of) a screen 100% -> 600% over 8f with motion blur, so the screen's picture becomes
the frame." B2 (out): "the GTA 6 frame shrinks into the screen of a 1990s CRT monitor"; B15 (in): "zoom through the
cabinet's screen into full-frame Race and Chase gameplay (R15.1)". SFX: S16 peak at the B15 zoom's midpoint.

**Values:** scale 100% <-> 600% over 8f about the origin P = (S c - F0)/(S - 1) (c = screen centre, F0 = frame
centre), so at 600% the screen's centre is the frame's centre. Curve (**kit choice**, the plan names no ease): scale
interpolated in log space (a zoom is read by its ratio per frame) with a quadratic ease, in = accelerating
(GSAP `power1.in`), out = decelerating (`power1.out`):

| frame | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| out | 600% | 394% | 274% | 201% | 157% | 129% | 112% | 103% | 100% |
| in | 100% | 103% | 112% | 129% | 157% | 201% | 274% | 394% | 600% |

Measured in the demo from crisp edges: within 0.2% of these values, positions within 1 px (`review/X6_*`).

**Screen geometry.** `screen: {rect:[x,y,w,h]}` or `{corners:[[x,y]TL,TR,BR,BL]}` = the screen picture on the zoom's
first frame, in frame px (measure it AFTER any push-in; stop or include the push inside the world). At 600% the
screen covers the frame only if it is >= 320 x 180 (`zoomGeometry().fill <= 6`; the helper warns otherwise; `scale:'fill'`
uses the exact cover scale instead of 600%, a deviation). For a seamless hand-off the picture rect should be exactly
320 x 180 (1/6 of the frame): then at 600% the picture IS the full frame, pixel for pixel, so
* B2 out: the beat's head handle and first frame (world at 600%) match B1's last full-frame GTA 6 frame (same
  R-ID, `data-media-start` continued; demo f434 vs f435). On a 4:3 CRT screen that means the 16:9 picture letterboxed
  (320 x 180 inside a 320 x 240 screen), as a 1990s CRT shows 16:9.
* B15 in: at 600% the cabinet picture is the full frame; keep the world (or cut to a plain full-frame R15.1 at the
  same media time one frame later, invisible; demo f538/f539). A perspective (corners) screen is not axis-aligned at
  600%: cut to the plain full-frame clip on the landing frame + 1.

**Sharpness: lay the screen picture out at 6x (`zoomFit`).** Media inside a box that GSAP later magnifies is rasterised
at its LAYOUT size; a media-treated `<video>` (CRT look, blur control) in a 320 px box is a 6x upscale at 600%
(blocky, measured). `X.zoomFit(box, screen, {res: 6})` lays the box out at 6x its on-screen size and maps it onto the
screen (rect: `scale(1/6)`; corners: a `matrix3d` homography), so it is 1:1 at 600%. It returns the rest size
`{w, h}`: compute media-treatment values from it (e.g. `RCK.motifs.crtGrading({w, h})`, all its values are relative).
For B2 use `zoomFit(..., {corners}, {res: 6})` INSTEAD of `RCK.motifs.crtFit` for the L8 insert.

**Motion blur** (**kit choice** of method; the built-in `data-motion-blur` does not detect scale-only movers, tested:
`movers=0`): every `<video>/<img>` in the world that carries a media-treatment blur control gets one
`--hf-color-grading-blur` value per frame (finite GSAP keyframes, the documented seek-safe contract). Model: trailing
180-degree shutter, radial streak at 540 px from the zoom centre, sigma = streak/sqrt(12), cap 24 px on screen,
divided by the element's on-screen scale and mapped through the **calibrated** value -> px table (`BLUR_CAL`:
1.0 = 21.8 px, 0.8 = 14.6, 0.6 = 8.9, 0.5 = 5.2, 0.3 = 2.2). Frames at rest are sharp (out: frame 0 and 8+; in: frame 0
and 9+); the fastest frames blur most. Non-media elements inside the world (bezel, a label) can take the same blur as a
CSS `filter: blur()` via `blurDom`. Prerequisite, once per medium (CLI):
`npx --yes hyperframes@0.9.52 media-treatment --selector "#b15-plate" --grading '{"effects":{"blur":0.01}}' --apply`
(a non-neutral payload; `blur` 0 alone is cleared as neutral) and write `style="--hf-color-grading-blur: 0"` on the
medium (sharp at rest). It reads as a defocus that peaks with the zoom speed, not a radial streak.

**Markup:**
```html
<div id="b15-world" class="rc-x6" style="z-index:2">                         <!-- untimed; GSAP scales it -->
  <video id="b15-plate" class="clip rc-full" src="assets/footage/R15.3.mp4" muted playsinline
         data-start="..." data-duration="..." style="--hf-color-grading-blur: 0"></video>
  <div id="b15-screen" class="rc-x6__screen">                                <!-- untimed; zoomFit lays it out -->
    <video id="b15-pic" class="clip" src="assets/footage/R15.1.mp4" muted playsinline
           data-start="..." data-duration="..." style="--hf-color-grading-blur: 0"></video>
  </div>
</div>
```
**Call:** `X.zoomFit('#b15-screen', { rect: [x, y, 320, 180] }, { res: 6 });`
`X.zoom(tl, '#b15-world', 166/30, { screen: { rect: [x, y, 320, 180] }, dir: 'in' })` -> `174/30`.
B2: `X.zoom(tl, '#b02-world', 6/30, { screen: { corners: [...] }, dir: 'out' })` -> `14/30`; before frame 6 the world
holds 600% (head handle = B1's picture continued).

**Beats:** B2 (b02-bug) beat f0-8 = local 6-14 (out). B15 (b15-proof) beat f160-168 = local 166-174 (in), S16 peak on
the midpoint beat f164; full-frame R15.1 from beat f168/169.

**Limitations:** the blur is a defocus, not a streak. Captions, the T9 label etc. that must not zoom stay OUTSIDE the
world. A CRT grading on the B2 insert (bulge, scanlines) is visible at 600% on B2's first frame, so that frame would
not match B1's clean last frame. Animating `--hf-color-grading-intensity` does NOT help: measured on 0.9.52, intensity
0 and 1 give the same blur (22.3 px) and the same scanlines (it does not scale effects). What works: a second, untreated
`<video>` of the same picture (same src/start/media-start, blur control only) on top of the CRT one inside the same
`zoomFit` box, faded 1 -> 0 (opacity on the clip, allowed) over the zoom's last frames, so the clean picture shrinks
into the monitor and the CRT look settles as it lands. B2 builder's call.

## X7 Light-leak dissolve

**Plan:** "A 12-16f dissolve through a light-leak overlay (Screen blend)." Into the glow line; B16: "X7 light-leak
dissolve (RT6) to black for the T5 glow line."

**Values:** 14f (**kit choice** inside 12-16; `frames` 12-16, a warning outside). The outgoing picture's opacity goes
1 -> 0 linearly over the 14f (`from`), optionally the incoming 0 -> 1 (`to`). The leak (`mix-blend-mode: screen`)
rises 0 -> 0.9 over the first half (`sine.out`) and falls to 0 at the end (`sine.in`), peak on frame at+7, while its
three layers drift. **RT6 stand-in:** RT6 (light-leak footage) is not available here, so the leak is the registry block
`organic-light-leak-overlay` (3 drifting gradient layers, Screen) adapted: same geometry relative to the frame,
retinted to the palette's warm glow `#FFF6E0` (exactly like the T5 leak in type.css, so the hand-off into the T5 glow
line matches). When RT6 is available: put `<video class="clip rc-x7__footage" src="assets/footage/RT6.mp4" muted
playsinline ...>` inside the `.rc-x7` element; the helper then skips its layers and drives the same opacity curve.
