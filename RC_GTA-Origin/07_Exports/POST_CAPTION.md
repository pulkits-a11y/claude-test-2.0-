Sometimes the bug is the product.

In 1995, DMA Design's Race'n'Chase was a dull game about a cop chasing criminals. Then a bug made the police cars try to drive straight through you, and suddenly it was fun. The team flipped the game, and every GTA since has had you running.

Don't believe it? Open GTA Online and find the Race and Chase arcade machine.
